title=Sling Required
description=No sling. No Back slot. No more weapons magically sticking to your bare back.
description=
description=Ever wondered how your survivor keeps a baseball bat or crowbar strapped to their back with nothing holding it in place?
description=
description=[b]Sling Required[/b] makes the Back weapon hotbar slot depend on what your character is actually wearing. Equip a makeshift sling or suitable back-worn gear to carry a weapon there. Take it off, and the Back slot becomes unavailable.
description=
description=[h1]Features[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]No sling, no Back slot:[/b] Without qualifying equipment, the Back hotbar slot disappears and anything attached to it returns to your inventory.
description=[*][b]Make your own sling:[/b] Turn belts, rope, sheets, and other materials allowed by your sandbox settings into wearable makeshift slings. No extra ingredients required.
description=[*][b]Reuse your materials:[/b] Dismantle a makeshift sling to get the original item type back with its saved numeric condition.
description=[*][b]Use existing gear:[/b] Configure whether bandoliers, hiking backpacks, military backpacks, framepacks, school backpacks, and sling bags give access to the Back slot.
description=[*][b]Mod-friendly:[/b] Worn equipment from other mods that explicitly provides the Back attachment slot qualifies automatically.
description=[*][b]Sandbox customization:[/b] Choose which materials can be turned into slings and which types of back-worn gear qualify.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Sandbox Options[/h1]
description=Find the Sling Required page in Custom Sandbox settings. Crafting materials and qualifying back-worn equipment can be configured independently.
description=
