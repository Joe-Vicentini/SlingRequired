description=[h1]Features[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] No sling, no Back slot. Weapons attached there are returned to your inventory when you lose access.
description=[*] Craft a makeshift sling from a belt, rope, sheet or other materials enabled in the sandbox settings.
description=[*] Dismantle your makeshift sling to recover the original material and its saved condition.
description=[*] Configure which materials can become slings and which backpacks, bandoliers or sling bags qualify.
description=[*] Mod-friendly: worn equipment that explicitly provides the Back attachment slot qualifies automatically.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Configuration[/h1]
description=All settings are on the Sling Required page of the Custom Sandbox options. You can choose which crafting materials and back-worn equipment count.
description=
description=[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
description=Designed for Project Zomboid Build 42.
